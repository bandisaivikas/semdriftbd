# Code of Conduct

SemDriftBD is a research project. We want everyone who opens an issue, sends a pull request or asks a question here to be treated with respect.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to Spark, Kafka or drift detection.
- Give and accept technical criticism about the work, not the person.
- Credit other people's ideas, code and results.
- Assume good faith when something is unclear, and ask before concluding.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or jokes about any group of people.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting issues or discussions after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@bandisaivikas](https://github.com/bandisaivikas), through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block contributors who break this code of conduct.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
